# "Cinta itu perbuatan": emotional short #2

**Final:** `final/emotional_podcast_final.mp4`. 54.0 s, 1080×1920 (9:16), 30 fps, H.264 High CRF 18, AAC 192k 48 kHz, 62 MB.

## Analysis
| Question | Answer |
|---|---|
| Topic | Real love is shown through actions, not imitated words or performances on social media |
| Deepest message | The most romantic things are often unspoken. Others may not see them as romantic, but the person you love feels them |
| Strongest moment | The call and response: Dr. Satria, "Tapi kita memberikan sebuah… apa?"; Alice, "Sebuah perbuatan lah."; Dr. Satria, "Memberikan perbuatan. Betul." |
| Emotions | Recognition (*"that's me, copying"*), then reflection, then warmth and quiet relief |
| Arc | Hook (unspoken feeling), the copied world of social media, the turn (what your partner actually feels), the payoff (the answer is "perbuatan"), the ending line |
| Palette | **Cool and desaturated (0.72 saturation, blue tint) for social media**, neutral at the turn, **warm (amber, 1.0–1.02) for the real**. The colour itself tells the story, from "dark" to warm in the hope/healing sense |
| Motion | A thin phone outline in the empty upper-right space. A flower appears at "bunga", hearts multiply at "meniru", the screen greys out at "semu". Imitation words get echo-ghost copies. The phone disappears at the turn. A large "…apa?" followed by Alice's amber answer with an underline that draws in |
| Music | Original, made in code, different from short #1. A filtered Am pad with a soft "digital" pulse (sine plucks on a 0.5 s grid) for the copied world; warm F then C with piano at the turn; a near-silent breath after "…apa?"; a warm F(add9) swell on the answer and C on "Betul"; a C-major piano melody at the end |
| Slows down | After "…apa?": the music drops to 15% so the question hangs before Alice answers |
| Ending | "Romantis tidak harus dengan selalu berpuisi, tetapi dengan perbuatan…" fading to black, then a card with his words: *Romantis tidak harus selalu berpuisi, tetapi dengan perbuatan.* |

## Sound
- **Dialogue:** the same processing chain as the full edit. Alice's off-mic answer is raised by 9.5 dB before compression, which puts it about 4 dB under the doctor, a natural distance.
- **Sound design:** a breath at 0 s, a soft whoosh into the "screen" world, a reverse swell into the turn, a warm low hit on Alice's answer, wind at the end. Room tone throughout.
- **Mix:** music ducked about 17 dB under speech. Final level -13.8 LUFS, -1.1 dB peak, LRA 3.4.
